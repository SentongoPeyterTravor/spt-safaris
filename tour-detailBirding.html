Uganda Birding Safari

Home Tours Birding Tours
Overview

A Birdwatcher’s Paradise in Uganda

From the papyrus swamps of Mabamba to the forests of Semuliki, this birding safari offers exceptional opportunities to spot rare and endemic species in some of Uganda’s richest habitats.

Itinerary

Day by Day

01

Arrival in Kampala

Meet your guide and transfer to a comfortable hotel, with a short briefing on the birding route ahead.

02

Mabamba Swamp Birding

Spend the morning exploring the wetlands for the rare shoebill stork and many water birds.

03

Semuliki Forest Excursion

Travel west to the forest belt for forest specialists, hornbills, and colorful species.

04

Lake Victoria & Wetland Trails

Enjoy early birding walks and photography opportunities before returning to Kampala.

05

Departure

Wrap up the trip with a final birding session and transfer to your onward destination.

FAQs

Common Questions

Is this suitable for beginner birders?

Yes. Our guides tailor the pace to your experience level and make sure you get the most from each stop.

What species can I expect?

Expect to see shoebills, kingfishers, bee-eaters, hornbills, and many forest and wetland specialists.

Can the safari be extended?

Absolutely. We can add gorilla trekking, wildlife viewing, or lake excursions to create a longer Uganda trip.
