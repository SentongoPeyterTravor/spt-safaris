Overview

A Once-in-a-Lifetime Encounter

Trek through the misty, ancient rainforest of Bwindi Impenetrable National Park to spend a precious hour face-to-face with a habituated mountain gorilla family. This 4-day journey combines thrilling trekking, comfortable lodges, and the guidance of expert local trackers.

Itinerary

Day by Day

01

Arrival & Transfer to Bwindi

Fly or drive to Bwindi, settling into your lodge with views over the forest canopy.

02

Gorilla Trekking Day

An early briefing followed by your guided trek to track a mountain gorilla family in the wild.

03

Community Walk & Batwa Experience

Visit a local Batwa community and learn about traditional forest life and conservation.

04

Departure

Morning at leisure before transferring back to Kampala or onward to your next destination.

FAQs

Common Questions

How fit do I need to be for gorilla trekking?

A moderate fitness level is recommended. Treks can range from 1–6 hours through forest terrain; porters are available to assist.

Do I need a gorilla permit?

Yes — we secure your permit as part of the package price, subject to availability at time of booking.

What's the best time of year to go?

Dry seasons (June–August and December–February) offer the easiest trekking conditions.
