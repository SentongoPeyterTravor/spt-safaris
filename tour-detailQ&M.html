Murchison Falls National Park Safari

Home Tours Murchison Falls
Overview

The Roar of the Nile & Savannah Wilds

Journey to Uganda’s largest and oldest protected area, where the mighty Nile River forces its way through a narrow 7-meter cleft to create the world's most powerful waterfall. This 4-day safari combines thrilling savannah game drives, an iconic boat cruise to the bottom of the falls, and a chance to spot the "Big Five" in their natural habitat.

Itinerary

Day by Day

01

Journey to Murchison via Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary

Drive north from Kampala, stopping at the Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary for a guided walking safari to track wild rhinos. Afterward, proceed to Murchison Falls National Park and check into your lodge..

02

Savannah Game Drive & Nile Boat Cruise

Set out for an early morning game drive to spot lions, leopards, giraffes, elephants, and herds of buffalo. In the afternoon, enjoy a 3-hour launch cruise on the Nile to the base of the falls, viewing hippos and massive crocodiles along the banks.

03

Hike to the Top of the Falls & Delta Cruise

Hike up to the dramatic cliffside where the Nile crashes through the gorge to feel the thunderous spray firsthand. Later, take a tranquil boat cruise down towards the Nile Delta for exceptional birdwatching, including a chance to see the rare Shoebill Stork.

04

Morning Track & Return to Kampala

Enjoy a final early morning sunrise game drive across the savannah plains before embarking on your scenic drive back to Kampala, concluding your safari adventure.

Common Questions

What wildlife can I expect to see in Murchison Falls?

A moderate fitness level is recommended. Treks can range from 1–6 hours through forest terrain; porters are available to assist.

Is the hike to the top of the falls strenuous?

Yes — we secure your permit as part of the package price, subject to availability at time of booking.

What's the best time of year to go?

Dry seasons (June–August and December–February) offer the easiest trekking conditions.
