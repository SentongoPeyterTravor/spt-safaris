Queen Elizabeth National Park

One park, many moods: crater lakes at dawn, lions in the fig trees and the Kazinga Channel alive with hippos.

Field note 02

A safari with a change of scene every hour.

Queen Elizabeth rewards curiosity. Start on a game drive across the Kasenyi plains, trade dust for a boat on the Kazinga Channel, then watch the Rwenzori Mountains turn blue beyond the lake.

We leave room between sightings. That is where the park's smaller stories appear: a fish eagle's call, a mongoose crossing, a village tea stop.
