Uganda / The close-to-home wild

Lake Mburo National Park

A small park with a generous personality: zebra-dotted hills, papyrus channels and the rare pleasure of exploring on foot.

Field note 07

The park that changes how you safari.

Mburo is intimate and wonderfully tactile. Cycle past zebra, walk with a ranger, or take a quiet boat where the water gathers birds and crocodiles.

It is an ideal first or final Uganda stop, but it deserves more than a rushed overnight. SPT uses its smaller scale to create more ways to enter the wild.
