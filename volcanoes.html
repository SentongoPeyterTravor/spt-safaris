Enter a highland landscape where bamboo, volcanic slopes and mountain gorillas share the same cool morning air.

Field note 08

A mountain encounter with a softer edge.

Volcanoes National Park is compact, dramatic and deeply atmospheric. Trek for gorillas, search for golden monkeys, then return through Rwanda's clean hills and thoughtful hospitality.

We plan the logistics carefully so the experience feels calm: permits, transfers, packing guidance and a route that leaves room for Kigali or a longer Uganda crossing.
