About SPT Safaris

Your Trusted Gateway to East Africa's Wild Heart

SPT Safaris was founded on a simple belief: that nature has the power to restore us. Based in Rubaga Kampala, our team has guided thousands of travelers through Uganda's forests, Kenya's plains, and Tanzania's craters.

  • Hand-picked lodges & camps for every budget
  • Permits, transport & logistics fully managed
  • Small-group and private safari options
  • Carbon-conscious travel planning
