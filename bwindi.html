Uganda / The quiet encounter

Bwindi Impenetrable Forest

Walk into an ancient green world where the forest keeps its own time and a gorilla family may choose to let you into theirs.

Field note 01

Come for the gorillas. Leave carrying the forest.

Bwindi is not a wildlife drive with a guaranteed view. It is a patient, human-scale trek through steep rainforest, guided by people who read the smallest signs: a bent fern, a distant call, fresh knuckle prints.

SPT builds the day around your pace, fitness and curiosity, with time for the communities, food and craft that make the forest more than a backdrop.

Forest hours

Early starts, cool air and a trail chosen around the gorilla family's movement.

Human roots

Meet the people whose lives and livelihoods are tied to the forest edge.
