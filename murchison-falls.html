Murchison Falls

Watch the Nile squeeze through a cleft of rock, then widen into a quiet stage for elephants, hippos and open-sky sunsets.

Field note 05

A river safari with real force behind it.

Murchison has a satisfying rhythm: game drive on the northern bank, a boat upstream, then the thunder of the falls. The landscape changes as quickly as the river does.

We pair the headline moment with lesser-known stops and a pace that makes room for the Nile's birdlife and the people who live beside it.
