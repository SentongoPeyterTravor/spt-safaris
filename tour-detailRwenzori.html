Rwenzori Mountains Hiking Escape

Overview

Alpine Trails, Glacial Lakes & Pure Adventure

The Rwenzori Mountains are one of Africa's most dramatic hiking destinations, with misty peaks, glacier-fed lakes, and challenging trails that reward adventurers with spectacular views and unforgettable wilderness.

Itinerary

Day by Day

01

Arrival in Fort Portal

Travel to the foothills of the Rwenzoris and settle into a lodge before a short orientation walk.

02

Trail Trekking Day

Begin your hike through rainforest and moorland, with scenic viewpoints and a stop at mountain streams.

03

Return & Scenic Transfer

Enjoy a final morning walk, then return to Kampala or continue your East African journey.

FAQs

Common Questions

Is the hike suitable for beginners?

It is best for travelers with a reasonable fitness level and some hiking experience, as trails can be steep and muddy.

What should I pack?

Bring layered clothing, waterproof gear, hiking boots, a daypack, and sun protection for changing mountain weather.

Can this tour be combined with other Uganda safaris?

Yes, we frequently pair it with gorilla trekking, wildlife safaris, or cultural tours for a richer Uganda itinerary.
