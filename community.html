Our Commitment

Journey Beyond the Wild, For Good

At SPT Safaris, we believe that meaningful travel goes beyond game drives and scenic vistas. Every safari we design actively reinvests in the conservation of East Africa's fragile ecosystems and the communities that call these wild places home.

From funding anti-poaching patrols to building school classrooms near the parks, sustainability is not an afterthought — it's woven into every itinerary we create.

  • 5% of profits to conservation
  • Local employment & fair wages
  • Community school & health partnerships
What We Do

Our Impact Initiatives

Every journey with SPT Safaris contributes directly to these six pillars of responsible tourism.

Conservation

We partner with national parks and wildlife authorities to protect endangered species and restore critical habitats.

Education

We support local schools near the parks, providing supplies, scholarships, and environmental education programs.

Community Upliftment

From artisan markets to guide training, we create sustainable livelihoods for the communities we visit.

Clean Water

We fund borehole and water filtration projects in rural communities near Uganda's national parks.

Anti-Poaching

We support ranger patrols and anti-poaching units with equipment, training, and logistics.

Reforestation

Thousands of indigenous trees planted each year to restore degraded forest edges and corridors.

Want to travel responsibly?

Choose a safari that gives back. Our consultants will craft an unforgettable itinerary that supports conservation and uplifts local communities.
